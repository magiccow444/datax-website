Who we are

DataX is an interdisciplinary student organization at San Diego State University that brings together students interested in working with data. Our members come from a variety of majors, experience levels, and backgrounds, including data science, analytics, statistics, econometrics, machine learning, business intelligence, and related fields.

What we do

Three ways to get involved.

  • Guest Speakers

    We invite professionals, alumni, and industry experts to share their experiences, career advice, and insights into the many fields that work with data.

  • Workshops

    Hands-on sessions where you build technical, professional, and analytical skills you can use in coursework, internships, research, and your career. No prior experience needed.

  • Community and Collaboration

    Socials, collaborations, and shared learning that connect students across disciplines. DataX is open to students of all majors and experience levels.
